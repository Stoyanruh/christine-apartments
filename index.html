Historic. Central. West Side.

Live in Buffalo's Former Kurtzmann Piano Factory

Christine Apartments is a four-story brick building at the corner of Niagara Street and Pennsylvania Street. It opened in 1899 as the Kurtzmann piano factory. Today it holds 32 apartments on the Niagara Street corridor, a short drive from downtown Buffalo.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

526 Niagara LLC operates Christine Apartments at 526 Niagara Street, Buffalo, New York ("we" or "us"). This policy tells you what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Your unit number and lease information.
  • Messages, photos, and other details you send us.
  • Information you put on a rental application.

We do not collect personal information through this website.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and show apartments.
  • To manage leases and collect rent.
  • To schedule and complete repairs.
  • To send building notices and reminders.

Mobile phone numbers

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. We share mobile information only with the phone service that delivers our messages, and only to deliver them.

When we share other information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only when we need to:

  • With a contractor who does a repair in your unit (your name, unit, and phone number, to schedule the work).
  • With service providers that help us run the building, such as our accountant.
  • When the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it for your lease, our records, and the law.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete your information, email christineapartments@outlook.com.
